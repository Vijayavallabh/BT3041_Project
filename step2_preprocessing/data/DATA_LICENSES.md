# Data sources, licences and notices

This folder's `raw_small/` holds unmodified copies of small public input files, frozen so that
`scripts/00_download.sh` rebuilds the exact inputs (checksums in `SHA256SUMS.freeze`). The large inputs are
downloaded, not stored. Each source keeps its own terms; they are not covered by any licence for the project's code.

| Files | Source | Terms and attribution |
|---|---|---|

The results shown here are in whole or part based upon data generated by the TCGA Research Network: https://www.cancer.gov/tcga. |
